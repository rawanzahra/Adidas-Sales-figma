# Adidas Sales & Operating Profit Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Tableau Public** dashboard analyzing Adidas product sales performance, revenue channels, profit margins, 
and regional distributions across the United States for **2020 and 2021**.

## 📈 Key Visualizations & Features

1. **US Regional Sales Map:**
   * Interactive chloropleth map displaying total sales by US state/region.
   * Color gradients range from **$6M to $64M**, highlighting high-performing geographic territories.

2. **Sales Channel Breakdown (Horizontal Bar Chart):**
   * Compares performance across sales channels: **In-store**, **Outlet**, and **Online**.
   * Identifies total sales contribution for each distribution method.

3. **Yearly Growth Trend (2020 vs 2021 Line Chart):**
   * Displays year-over-year revenue growth and operating profit trajectories from **2020 to 2021**.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive parameters on the left pane:

* **Region:** Filter performance by geographic regions (`Midwest`, `Northeast`, `South`, `Southeast`, `West`).
* **Year of Invoice Date:** Filter by target years (`2020`, `2021`).
